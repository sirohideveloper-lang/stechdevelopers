Privacy Policy

S Tech Developers · Last updated: 12 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to all apps published by S Tech Developers on Google Play, including Smart Calculator, Invoice Generator and Expense Tracker.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Our apps do not require you to create an account, and they do not send any data to us or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

Some apps let you enter information such as calculations, invoice details (client names, items, amounts) or expense records. This information is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never uploaded, transmitted or backed up by us. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Internet access

Our apps work completely offline and do not connect to the internet to function.

Third-party services

Our apps do not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking tools.

Permissions

If an app requests a permission (for example, storage access to save or share an invoice as a PDF), it is used only for that feature and only on your device.

Children's privacy

Our apps are general-purpose utilities and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how our apps handle data, we will update this page and the date shown above.

Contact

For any questions about this policy or our apps, email us at sirohideveloper@gmail.com.
